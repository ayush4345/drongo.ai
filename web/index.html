Confidential agent payments

Agents pay agents. Privately.

Drongo meters thousands of agent-to-agent API calls off-chain, then settles them once on Stellar with a zero-knowledge proof — so usage volume, pricing and timing never touch the chain.

The problem

Metered machine commerce leaks everything.

Agents are starting to buy inference, data and compute from each other, and pay-per-use is the natural model. Settled naively on-chain, it becomes a metadata firehose.

01

Usage is competitive intel

Every micropayment on-chain reconstructs the usage graph — who pays whom, how much, how often.

02

Pricing is a secret

Providers give different rates to different customers. Per-call amounts on-chain expose the rate card.

03

Per-call settlement is wasteful

Posting thousands of micro-transactions is slow and costly. You want to meter freely and settle once.

How it works

Meter off-chain. Settle once. Prove it.

A payment channel: the consumer signs cumulative EdDSA-BabyJubjub vouchers as it consumes, and a single Groth16 proof settles the channel on Stellar — asserting the payout is correctly derived from a counter-signed meter at the agreed rate.
