The problem

Metered machine commerce leaks everything.

As agents start buying inference, data, and compute from each other, pay-per-use is the natural model. Done naively on-chain, it's a metadata firehose.

01

Usage is competitive intel

Every micropayment on-chain reconstructs the full usage graph — who pays whom, how much, how often.

02

Pricing is secret

Providers give different rates to different customers. Per-call amounts on-chain expose the entire rate card.

03

Per-call settlement is wasteful

Posting thousands of micro-transactions is slow and expensive. You want to meter freely and settle once.

How it works

Meter off-chain. Settle once. Prove it.

Drongo runs the meter as a payment channel: the consumer signs cumulative EdDSA-BabyJubjub vouchers as it consumes, and a single Groth16 proof settles the channel on Stellar — asserting the payout is correctly derived from a counter-signed meter at the agreed rate, without revealing the calls, the count, or the rate.

Public on-chain
  • Channel open / close
  • Escrow amount
  • Settlement amount
  • Nullifier & rate commitment
Private — never revealed
  • Number of calls / units
  • The per-unit rate
  • Per-call timing & frequency
  • Which services were used
Features

A private toll-gate for autonomous agents.

🛡️

Confidential metering

Usage volume, rate, and timing stay off-chain. The ledger only ever sees one settlement.

⚡

Thousands of calls, one tx

Meter freely off-chain; settle the whole channel in a single Stellar transaction.

🔏

ZK-proven correctness

A Groth16 proof verified inside a Soroban contract guarantees the payout matches the signed meter.

🎟️

Escrow & gatekeeping

A prepaid USDC ceiling caps spend; the provider serves only against fresh in-budget vouchers, and halts otherwise.

🚪

x402-native front door

Discovery and channel-open ride Stellar's x402 — interoperable with the agentic-commerce ecosystem.

🤖
